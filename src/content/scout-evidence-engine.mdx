*Part of [Tools for Operating Voice Agents](/projects/voice-ai-platform), for investigating patterns across calls.*

## From search to an investigation

A dashboard can reveal that transfers increased without showing the repeated interaction behind the change. Scout takes a question, searches calls by wording and meaning, groups related cases, maps conversation paths and reads selected calls turn by turn. It produces candidate explanations with links to the exact evidence it inspected.

## Spend effort on verification

I first compared representations for finding similar calls. Simple features derived from what the agent did clustered calls about twice as consistently as model-written labels, and the labels did not reveal additional patterns in that experiment. That result led me to favor measured runtime behavior over extra generated descriptions.

In a separate benchmark, a first pass produced 74 candidate positives; an independent model review retained 34. A second model reviewed the results. We did not measure agreement with human reviewers. Scout therefore checks each proposed citation against the call and turn data it read. It rejects inaccessible calls, unseen turns, mismatched quotes, wrong speakers and invalid turn order before presenting the report.

The report keeps population, examined, matched and read counts separate. A literal search can find many similar phrases without showing that the behavior is prevalent. Scout's prevalence measurement still has known gaps, including cases where an incomplete measurement can produce a misleading zero.

## Making investigations usable

The first cohort build took 149 seconds server-side. After precomputing a runtime profile beside each call's embedding, two checks on the same 9,824-call selection completed in 27.92 and 27.72 seconds including CLI polling. Those timing boundaries differ, so I do not treat them as an exact speedup ratio. Hosted search later stalled on cold caches and loaded calls sequentially; warming the index and batching reads brought three failing searches down to 3–4.5 seconds.

I also fixed a failure-isolation bug: one call rejected by a privacy check could escape its handler and terminate the rest of a batch. A synthetic three-call reproduction showed the behavior; isolating each call kept one rejection from cancelling its neighbors.

## Status and limits

Scout runs in our main production environment and is available to internal staff through its CLI and powers investigations inside Platform Chat. The viewer above replays a fictional investigation in the report interface. It does not connect to the service.

Citation checks establish that evidence exists and was available to the investigator; they do not establish that an interpretation is correct. The research comparison used a second model rather than human reviewers, so accuracy against people is unmeasured. Prevalence measurement is still being improved, and a citation-backed observation should not be read as a confirmed cause or supported problem unless the required checks are present.
