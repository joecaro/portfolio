## Client CRM

Our support team tracked client reporting progress in spreadsheets. I built this app to give the team one place to update each client's status and contact details.

Staff can group records, add notes and completion dates, view status totals, and export the current list as a spreadsheet. The app uses a Next.js interface, an Express API, MongoDB, and JWT authentication.
